# Xian Bai News Gathering

This module collects, filters, ranks, summarizes, and delivers
news that may be interesting to the user.

## Current status

Early development.

## Planned capabilities

- Collect news from external sources
- Detect new articles
- Remove duplicates
- Store articles
- Identify potentially interesting stories
- Learn user interests
- Summarize relevant stories
- Explain why a story may matter
- Deliver personalized news through Xian Bai

## Design principle

Use existing libraries and services wherever practical.
Build custom components only when Xian Bai actually needs them.
